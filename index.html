Fabric first

Three knits, developed
and re-developed.

We did not want a catalogue of fabrics. We wanted three that earn their place — one compressive, one soft, one warm — and then we built almost everything from them.

A dense recycled-nylon knit with real recovery. Squat-proof at every size we cut, and the base of the Airlift Legging, Halo Bra, Reset Short and Studio Flare.
Pima cotton blended with modal and brushed on the inside face. It is the reason the Cloudset Crop Tee sells out first every drop.
Knit on a narrow gauge so the rib reads as texture rather than gym kit. Opaque under stretch, soft enough to sleep in.
