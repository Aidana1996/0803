Your fitting room

Our story

The hour between things.

0803 started because of a specific, unglamorous problem: nothing in the closet worked for the whole day. There was gym kit, and there was everything else, and getting from one to the other meant a change of clothes and usually a change of plan.

So we started with three fabrics instead of a mood board. One compressive knit that holds through a class and reads as trousers in a restaurant. One soft knit with enough weight to hang properly. One warm layer that does not add a size. Everything on the floor is cut from those three, in the same seven colours, so the pieces work together without being a uniform.

Why a store first

Two in five pieces of women's apparel bought online come back, almost always for fit. We did not want to build a business on that. We wanted a room where you could put the thing on, hinge forward, sit down, look in a real mirror, and know.

A shipping label cannot tell you the rise is wrong. A customer standing in front of a mirror will.

So the website you are reading is a window, not a till. It tells you what is on the rack, in your size, today. You reserve it, we hang it in a room, and you decide in person. Online ordering will come — after we are confident you can pick your size without thinking about it.

The name

8:03 is the minute the light gets good on Red Hill Avenue, and it is also the class most of us are late to. It seemed like the right thing to name a store after.

How we make decisions

Two jobs, or it doesn't get made.

Every piece has to work in at least two settings before we cut it. The Studio Flare is a legging and a trouser. The Rib Longline is a bra and a top. The Sunday Rib Dress is the whole outfit.

It is a narrow rule and it kills a lot of good-looking samples. It also means the floor stays small enough that we can tell you honestly what is worth your money.
