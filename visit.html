Getting here

Driving: Exit Red Hill from the CA-55, head south. We are on the west side, past Alton. Free surface lot with about forty spaces.

Transit: OCTA Route 76 stops at Red Hill & Alton, a three-minute walk.

Accessibility: Step-free entry from the lot, a 36" doorway, one accessible fitting room and an accessible restroom.

Phone: (714) 555-0803

What we do in store

Things a website cannot do for you.

01

Styling hour

Tell us what the week looks like and we pull a rack before you arrive. Sixty minutes, free, and you can buy nothing.

02

Hemming while you wait

Free on full-price trousers and leggings, $18 otherwise. Most are done in about twenty minutes.

03

Fit checks

Bring in a piece you already own and we will tell you honestly whether ours is any better for you.

04

Repairs

Seams, cuffs and waistbands on anything with our label, free for the first year.

Appointments

Book an hour with the floor to yourself.

Weekday mornings and Sunday evenings are quietest. Tell us roughly what you are after and we will have a rack waiting.

Prefer to talk? Call (714) 555-0803 during opening hours.

We reply within one business day.
